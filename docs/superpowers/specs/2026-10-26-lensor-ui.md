# Lensor UI

As of 2026-10-26. This file records the approved UI design. It adds to `2026-10-26-lensor-design.md`. When the two files differ about the UI, this file wins.

The design canvas is at <https://claude.ai/artifact/STw5f6ntxjZ3EsrdCMJR2h>. The canvas is private. Its figures are sample data.

## 1. Principles

- Write for a person who is not a lawyer. Use plain names first. Show the CUAD name second, in small grey text.
- Show one main point for each section. Put technical details in a closed `<details>` element.
- The UI does not interpret the law. It says what Lensor found, for example "Lensor found no clause that limits this."
- Each page shows the notice "Lensor helps you read contracts faster. It does not give legal advice."

## 4. Risk score

The score is a sum of points, from 0 to 100. Each rule is a fact that the extraction found, so the UI can show each reason.

| Rule | Points | Reason text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uncapped Liability found | 30 | No limit on what you could owe for some claims |
| Cap on Liability not found | 15 | No general limit on what you could owe |
| Renewal Term found | 15 | The contract renews by itself |
| Non-Compete found | 15 | Limits work with competitors |
| Change of Control found | 15 | Rules apply if a company is sold |
| Termination for Convenience found | 10 | A party can end the contract without a reason |

Risk level: high at 60 or more, medium from 30 to 59, low below 30.

## 5. Shared parts

- **Header:** cells split by vertical hairlines. Logo cell, a tagline cell ("Contract review, checked against lawyer labels"), the nav cells Contracts, Ask and Accuracy (label at the bottom of the cell; the current cell has weight 600 and a 7px blue square), and a blue "Upload a contract" button.
- **Arrow button:** a label and a 52px square tile with a diagonal arrow. Black button with a blue tile, or blue button with a white tile.
- **Pill:** risk level. High is red filled, Medium is outlined, Low is grey, "Not found" is dashed.
- **Black card:** a title, rows of a large value and a short label split by hairlines, and a full-width arrow button. It overlaps a grey to white band.
- **Section heading:** a small blue label, a centred heading, and one line of grey text.
- **Icon card:** a white card with a 32px blue icon tile, a title, a short text, and an optional link.
- **Footer:** the notice and the CUAD credit (The Atticus Project, CC BY 4.0).

## 6. Pages

| Route | Name | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Contracts | Hero "Your contracts, checked for risk.", a black card with three numbers (high-risk contracts, contracts with "No limit", contracts with "Automatic renewal") and "Ask about these contracts", the 10 clause names, risk filter tabs with counts, a grid of contract cards (score, level, up to three reasons, "Open the contract"), and a black band that explains the risk score |
| `/upload` | Upload | A large "Upload" title and a column of facts (what it does, time, files, not supported). States: idle (drop zone, file input, "Use a sample contract"), working (file name, progress bar, the four steps Read, Split, Index, Find clauses), done (the result card and "Open the contract"), error (the server message and "Choose another file") |
| `/contracts/:id` | Contract viewer | A large title and a black risk card. "What to watch": up to three icon cards for the highest-risk clauses. The contract text with marks in the family colours. A panel with all 10 clauses as an accordion (plain name, CUAD name, pill, and when open: quotes, summary, "Show in the contract"). A missing clause shows "Not found". |
| `/contracts/:id?from=ask#<start>` | Viewer from a source | The same page. A blue bar says "You opened this from an answer" and links "Back to the answer". The clause at `<start>` is open, has an outline, and is in view. |
| `/ask` | Ask | Hero "Ask about your contracts". When empty, three example questions as icon cards. Each question shows the user text, then an answer card: a status pill, "N of 8 steps", each tool step in plain words, the streamed answer, and the sources as grey cards that link to the viewer. An unverified source shows red text "Not found in the contract text" and no link. The composer has a text area and an "Ask" or "Stop" button. |
| `/eval` | Accuracy | Hero "How often Lensor gets it right.", a black card with the overall score (macro F1 × 100), precision and recall as "N in 100", and a column with the run facts (contracts, model, mode, prompt version, date, cost). A list of the 10 clause types sorted by score, with a bar and a label: Strong at 80 or more, Good from 70 to 79, "Check by hand" below 70. A black band with the command and the raw metrics. With no run, the page says "No evaluation run yet" and shows the command. |

The chat state lives above the routes, so "Back to the answer" shows the same conversation.
